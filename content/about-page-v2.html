Can TorlyAI guarantee visa approval?

No. TorlyAI provides guidance and preparation tools, not legal advice or approval guarantees.

What TorlyAI IS:

  • AI-powered analysis based on official Home Office guidelines
  • Probability indicator for endorsement and visa success
  • Identification of application strengths and weaknesses
  • Preparation tool to improve application quality

What TorlyAI is NOT:

  • Legal immigration advice (consult a qualified immigration lawyer for legal advice)
  • Guarantee of visa approval
  • Substitute for endorsing body or UKVI decision
  • Official UK government service

Final decisions are made by:

  • Endorsing body: Approves or rejects your endorsement application
  • UK Visas & Immigration (UKVI): Approves or rejects your visa application

TorlyAI maximizes your chances of success but cannot guarantee outcomes. We recommend consulting a qualified immigration advisor for complex cases.
