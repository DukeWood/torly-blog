Visa Eligibility Questions

What is the UK Innovator Founder Visa?

The UK Innovator Founder Visa (2026) is an immigration route for experienced entrepreneurs who want to establish an innovative, viable, and scalable business in the United Kingdom.

Key Requirements:

  • Investment: Minimum £50,000
  • Endorsement: Required from one of 8 authorized bodies
  • Innovation: Business must be new to UK market
  • Duration: 3 years initially, renewable
  • Settlement: Eligible for permanent residence after 3 years

Note: Previously called "Innovator Visa" before 2023 reform.

Source: UK Home Office (gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa)

Who is eligible for the UK Innovator Founder Visa?

You're eligible if you meet ALL of these criteria:

  • At least 18 years old
  • Have a genuine, innovative business idea that's new to the UK market
  • Can invest at least £50,000 in your business
  • Can demonstrate business experience or skills
  • Meet English language requirements (B2 level)
  • Have sufficient personal savings (minimum £1,270)
  • Can secure endorsement from an approved body

Can I switch to Innovator Founder Visa from inside the UK?

Yes! You can switch to an Innovator Founder Visa from certain visa categories while remaining in the UK:

  • Allowed: Skilled Worker, Start-up, Graduate, Visitor (in some cases)
  • Not allowed: Short-term visitor, seasonal worker, domestic worker

Switching from inside the UK means you don't need to return to your home country to apply.

What if my business idea isn't "innovative" enough?

"Innovative" doesn't mean world-first invention. According to Home Office guidance, innovation means:

  • New to the UK market (not necessarily globally unique)
  • Different from existing competitors (unique selling proposition)
  • Uses technology or processes differently
  • Improves on existing solutions

Example: A delivery app with AI route optimization would be innovative even though delivery apps exist.

Do I need business experience to qualify?

While not officially required by Home Office, endorsing bodies typically expect:

  • Previous startup or business management experience, OR
  • Relevant industry expertise (5+ years), OR
  • Strong track record in your field with demonstrable achievements

First-time entrepreneurs with strong industry expertise are regularly approved.

Application Process Questions

How long does the UK Innovator Founder Visa application process take?

Total Timeline: 18-24 weeks

Stage 1: Endorsement (6-8 weeks)

  • Prepare business plan and documents: 2-4 weeks
  • Submit to endorsing body: Immediate
  • Endorsing body review: 4-6 weeks
  • Interview (if required): 1-2 weeks wait time

Stage 2: Visa Application (12-16 weeks)

  • Complete visa application: 1 week
  • Submit and pay fees: Immediate
  • Biometrics appointment: 1-2 weeks
  • UKVI processing: 8-12 weeks
  • Decision and visa issuance: 1 week

TorlyAI helps you prepare Stage 1 documents in days, not weeks.

What documents do I need for the application?

For Endorsement Stage:

  • Comprehensive business plan (typically 20-30 pages)
  • Financial projections (3-5 years)
  • Evidence of investment funds (£50,000+)
  • CV/resume demonstrating experience
  • Market research and competitive analysis
  • Pitch deck (10-15 slides)

For Visa Application:

  • Valid passport
  • Endorsement letter from approved body
  • English language certificate (B2 level)
  • TB test certificate (if from required country)
  • Bank statements (maintenance funds)
  • Criminal record certificate (if required)

Can I reapply if my endorsement is rejected?

Yes! You can reapply:

  • Same endorsing body: Usually after 6 months, after addressing rejection reasons
  • Different endorsing body: Immediately, if your business fits their criteria better

Common rejection reasons include:

  • Insufficient innovation demonstration
  • Weak financial projections
  • Unclear market opportunity
  • Poor interview performance

Costs & Financial Questions

How much does the UK Innovator Founder Visa cost in total?

Total Cost Breakdown (2026):

  • Visa application fee: £1,191
  • Immigration Health Surcharge: £1,035/year × 3 years = £3,105
  • Minimum business investment: £50,000
  • Endorsement body fee: £500 - £1,500
  • English language test: £150 - £200
  • TB test (if required): £100 - £150
  • Professional services (optional): £2,000 - £5,000

Minimum Total: £54,796 - £55,796

Realistic Total (with professional help): £57,000 - £61,000

Source: UK Home Office (January 2026)

Can I use borrowed money for the £50,000 investment?

Yes, with conditions:

  • Bank loans: Allowed if secured against assets
  • Family gifts: Allowed with gift letter and proof of funds
  • Investor funding: Allowed with investment agreement
  • NOT allowed: Unsecured personal loans without assets

You must prove:

  • Funds are genuinely available
  • You have legal right to use them
  • Funds will be invested in UK business

Do I need to create jobs immediately?

No immediate job creation required. However:

At endorsement stage:

  • Show POTENTIAL for job creation in business plan
  • Demonstrate scalability of business model

At renewal (3 years):

  • Must show significant progress
  • Job creation helps demonstrate growth
  • Alternative: revenue growth, customer acquisition, funding raised

Most successful applicants project 5-10 jobs within 3 years.

Endorsing Body Questions

Which endorsing bodies are authorized for UK Innovator Founder Visa?

There are 8 authorized endorsing bodies (2026):

  1. Tech Nation - Technology and digital businesses
  2. Innovate UK - Innovation across all sectors
  3. The Global Entrepreneurs Programme - High-growth businesses
  4. Envestors Limited - Investment-ready businesses
  5. UK Endorsing Services (UKES) - General businesses
  6. British Business Bank - Scalable businesses
  7. CityFibre - Infrastructure and connectivity
  8. London & Partners - London-based businesses

TorlyAI recommends the best fit based on your industry and business stage.

How do I choose the right endorsing body?

Consider these factors:

1. Industry Focus:

  • Tech/digital → Tech Nation or Innovate UK
  • General business → UKES or Global Entrepreneurs
  • Investment-backed → Envestors or British Business Bank

2. Success Rate:

  • Tech Nation: 75-80%
  • Innovate UK: 80-85%
  • UKES: 70-75%

3. Fees:

  • Range: £500 - £1,500
  • Check current fees on each body's website

4. Processing Time:

  • Fastest: 4-6 weeks (Tech Nation, Global Entrepreneurs)
  • Slowest: 8-10 weeks (Innovate UK, British Business Bank)
