Mobile Menu Test

Resize your browser to mobile width (below 768px) to test the hamburger menu.

Instructions:

  1. Click the hamburger button (three lines) in the top right
  2. Menu should slide in from the right
  3. Click outside or press Escape to close
  4. Hamburger lines should animate to X when open
